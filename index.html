Everything is spotless and well stocked when you arrive, down to a welcome hamper with eggs and vegetables we grow next door.

Before you book

What guests ask us most. Facts checked 26 September 2026.

It's on the edge of Ferring, not deep countryside.

Our lane starts at the main road, with Asda across it. Guests tell us they barely notice the road once they're at the lodge.

Our greenhouses are next door.

You may see us at work there in the day. The lodge has its own private, fenced garden.

The beach isn't on the doorstep.

It's 5 to 8 minutes by car, or a 25 to 35 minute walk along the Rife.

How many people does it sleep?
Up to 6, in two king-size bedrooms and a twin room. A travel cot and highchair are free on request.
Is the hot tub private? Does it cost extra?
It is private to the lodge, under its own covered gazebo in your garden, and included in the price. You can use it in the rain.
Check-in and check-out?
Check-in from 3pm, check-out by 10am. Self check-in with a lockbox. Ask us if you need other times.
Can we bring a dog?
No pets. Ask us about assistance animals before booking.
How far is the beach?
5 to 8 minutes by car, or 25 to 35 minutes on foot along the Ferring Rife.
Is it step-free?
Inside is all on one level, with a large wet room and widened doorways. There are 2 steps to the front door; we put a ramp down on request.
7 more questions
Is there parking?
Yes, free: 2 cars at the lodge, with more space on the nursery.
How do we book?
Choose dates and book on Airbnb, or book direct with us: ring or text 07940 796860, or email info@highdownhideaways.com.
What is provided?
Bedding, towels, bathrobes, beach towels, toiletries, Wi-Fi, a full kitchen with coffee pods, and a welcome hamper with eggs from our hens and produce in season.
Is there Wi-Fi and TV?
Fast Wi-Fi throughout. A 50-inch smart TV in the lounge (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video) and a 40-inch smart TV in every bedroom.
Is there air conditioning?
Not yet. Fans are provided for hot nights.
Parties or smoking?
No parties, events or smoking.
How do we get there without a car?
Goring-by-Sea station is 1.5 miles away (about 28 minutes on foot); Angmering station is 1.9 miles.
